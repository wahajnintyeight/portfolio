# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams evaluating Muhammad Wahaj for software developer roles.

## Product Purpose

A professional portfolio that helps recruiters assess fit through real project work, production experience, technical range, and a direct way to get in touch.

## Positioning

The portfolio showcases developer tools and web products through real project screens, concise context, and optional implementation details.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan selected projects, experience, skills, and education before deciding whether to contact Wahaj about a role. They may browse from a phone or desktop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A single-page React and TypeScript site with Work, Experience, Stack, Education, and Contact sections.
- Project screens open in an image carousel; external project and profile links remain available.
- Preserve existing facts, project descriptions, working links, portrait, and screenshots. Do not add unverified achievements or claims.
- The responsive site must remain readable and navigable on small screens, including its requested mobile bottom navigation.

## Brand Commitments

- The portfolio represents Muhammad Wahaj, Software Developer.
- The user requested a dark, modern developer-oriented design and a mobile app-like view with bottom navigation.
- The latest palette follows the navy suit and blue tie in the portrait: #0D1422, #18263B, #344B68, #91B6E6, and #E3EBF5. This supersedes the earlier plum palette.
- The opening presents the person and their work. Resume statistics belong only in relevant supporting details.
- The linked digital resume is a structural reference, not a request to reproduce its pixels.

## Evidence on Hand

- Profile, experience, skills, education, project descriptions, and contact links: `constants.ts`.
- Project screenshots: `assets/thumbnails/`.
- Transparent portrait: `assets/profile-cutout.png`; display it with slightly darker highlights to integrate with the page.
- Career figures already present in the portfolio include 4+ years, 30+ applications, 900K+ investor records, 40% lower P95 load time, and 1,000+ concurrent GPS streams.

## Product Principles

1. Let real work introduce the developer.
2. Make technical depth quick to scan and easy to inspect.
3. Keep every claim tied to the existing evidence.
